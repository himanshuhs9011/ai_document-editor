01Why an AI document editor

Most AI writing today happens in a chat window: you copy text out of your document, paste it into a chatbot, then copy the answer back and fix the formatting it broke. The AI never sees the whole document, never knows your other files, and every change is all-or-nothing. This editor puts the AI where the writing already is.

It sees your document

The assistant reads the document you have open, its structure and its formatting, so “make the budget section formal” just works.

You stay the editor

AI edits arrive as suggestions. Accept or reject each paragraph; unchanged text keeps its formatting; one undo puts everything back.

Your files stay yours

Documents and accounts live on your PC. Text goes only to the AI provider you pick, with your key, when you use AI.

02Why AI belongs in it

The slow part of writing is rarely typing. It is the blank page, the rewrite in a different tone, finding the one figure buried in a 90-page contract, and checking a draft before it goes out. Those are the jobs the AI does here, each with its own tool.

04The AI features, one at a time

Pick a feature to see what it does, when to use it, how many AI requests it makes, and how to use it.

AI feature

Chat and review

The assistant on the right reads your open document. Questions are answered in the chat; requests to change the document come back as suggested changes you review before anything is applied.

Use it when
“Make this more formal”, “add a section on risks”, “what does clause 4 mean?”
AI requests
1 per message

How to use it

  1. Type in the message box at the bottom of the assistant and press Enter.
  2. For an edit, a review panel opens above the document with each change marked word by word.
  3. Accept or reject each change, or click Accept all. One undo (Ctrl+Z) reverts the whole edit.
  4. Select any text to get quick actions too: Improve Grammar, Make Concise, Add Detail, Formal or Friendly Tone, Summarize.

/ commands

Type / in any paragraph for a menu of AI actions and quick blocks. The AI works on that paragraph and shows its result in a card before it touches your text.

AI actions
continue · improve · fix grammar · shorter · longer · formal · translate · summarize · table · ask
AI requests
1 per command

How to use it

  1. In the paragraph you want to change, type / and a few letters to filter, e.g. /impr.
  2. Press Enter. Add words for the commands that take them: /translate hindi, /table monthly budget by team, /ask write an intro.
  3. The result streams into a card: click Replace or Insert below.

Studio: a writing team

Pick a profession and a team of AI agents writes the whole document. A lead editor plans the sections, a specialist writes each one in its own craft, reviewers check the draft against their checklist, and flagged sections are revised. You watch it happen.

Professions
legal · medical · reports · research · news · articles · social media · film & TV · YouTube · reels · courses
Use it when
You need a complete first draft: a contract, a report, a script, a course outline.
AI requests
Draft: 1 + 1 per section
Review and revise: up to 1 + 2 per section + 1 per reviewer

How to use it

  1. Click the chat options under the message box (This document · Quick) and choose Write a whole document.
  2. Pick a profession on the left and a document type, and answer the brief.
  3. Optionally tick source files: facts from them are cited as [1], [2] and listed under Sources.
  4. Choose Draft, or Draft, review and revise for the reviewers’ pass, then Write it.
  5. When it’s done, Add to my document. If the document isn’t empty, it arrives as a review.

Who works on a report

lead editor     plans the sections and assigns each one
specialists     each writes the sections in its craft
reviewers       check claims, gaps and clarity
→ revise        flagged sections rewritten

Deep read (RLM, recursive language model)

Normal AI chat only sees the parts of a long document that fit in one prompt. Deep read gives the model the whole text as a variable in a sandbox. It writes small programs to look around (search, slice, split into chunks), hands pieces to helper model calls, and combines what they find. Short texts (under about 24,000 characters) are simply read in one pass.

Use it when
“Summarise this 120-page report”, “list every deadline”, “compare section 2 with section 7”.
AI requests
1 for short texts
long texts: up to 8 steps + 24 helper reads

How to use it

  1. Open the long document.
  2. Click the chat options (This document · Quick) and pick Deep read under Reading mode.
  3. Ask your question. A line shows each step as it reads.
  4. Under the answer, Read with code folds out the exact code it ran and what it printed.

The code runs in a locked sandbox: no files, no network, no imports, with time and size limits.

What a step looks like

# the model writes this; the sandbox runs it
parts = chunks(context, 20000)
answers = llm_query_batch(
    ["List every deadline:\n" + p for p in parts])
print(len(parts), answers)
# next step: combine the answers and finish

Search across your documents (RAG, retrieval-augmented generation)

Every file you open or upload is split into passages. When you ask a question, the passages most relevant to it are found first, and only those are given to the AI, numbered. The answer cites them like [1] [2], so you can check every claim against the source.

Use it when
“What did we agree on payment terms?”, “which document mentions the Pune office?”
AI requests
1 per question

How to use it

  1. Open your files once with File → Open a file (Word, PDF, text).
  2. In the chat options, choose All my documents under Reads.
  3. Ask. Click a [1] in the answer, or a source under it, to see the exact passage.

In the Windows app, passages are ranked by keyword match, so use the words the document uses.

RAG or deep read?

search · rag   a fact that lives in a few passages,
               across many files; fast, 1 request

deep read      a question about the whole of one long
               document; thorough, several requests

Autocomplete

As you pause, the AI suggests how your sentence continues, in grey. Press Tab to take it, or keep typing to ignore it.

Use it when
Drafting quickly, when you know where a sentence is going.
AI requests
1 per suggestion

How to use it

  1. Open More tools (⋯) in the toolbar and turn on AI autocomplete.
  2. Write, and pause at the end of a sentence. Press Tab to accept the suggestion.
  3. It makes a request each time it suggests, so a free key’s per-minute limit runs out faster.

08Questions

Is it free?

Yes, and open source. AI usage is billed by your provider on your own key; Gemini’s free tier costs nothing.

AI says “quota exceeded”.

Free keys allow a few requests a minute. Wait a minute; Studio and deep read make several requests each, so heavy use suits a paid key.

Can I use my account on another PC?

Accounts belong to the PC they were made on. Copy the data folder to move your documents.

Mac or Linux?

Not yet as an installer. The source code runs anywhere Python and Node.js do.

Scanned PDFs?

Not yet in the Windows app: scanned pages import without text. PDFs with real text work.
