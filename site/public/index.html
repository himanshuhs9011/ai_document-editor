// the problem

Why an AI document editor

Most AI writing still happens in a chat window, away from the document. The AI never sees the whole file, never knows your other files, and every answer is all-or-nothing.

the chatbot way

  1. ✕Copy text out of your document
  2. ✕Paste it into a chatbot
  3. ✕Copy the answer back
  4. ✕Fix the formatting it broke
  5. ✕Repeat for every change

this editor

  1. ✓Ask right where you write
  2. ✓The AI sees the whole document
  3. ✓Changes are marked word by word
  4. ✓Accept or reject each one
  5. ✓Formatting kept; one undo puts it back

◉

It sees your document

Its structure and formatting too, so “make the budget section formal” just works.

✎

You stay the editor

AI edits arrive as suggestions. Nothing changes until you accept it.

⌂

Your files stay yours

Documents and accounts live on your PC. Text goes only to the AI provider you pick.

// how to use

The AI features, one at a time

Pick a feature: what it does, when to use it, how many AI requests it makes, and how to use it.

AI feature

feature 1 / 6

Chat and review

The assistant on the right reads your open document. Questions are answered in the chat; requests to change the document come back as suggested changes you review before anything is applied.

use it when
“Make this more formal”, “add a section on risks”, “what does clause 4 mean?”
AI requests
1 per message

how to use it

  1. 1Type in the message box at the bottom of the assistant and press Enter.
  2. 2For an edit, a review panel opens above the document with each change marked word by word.
  3. 3Accept or reject each change, or click Accept all. One undo (Ctrl+Z) reverts the whole edit.
  4. 4Select any text for quick actions too: Improve Grammar, Make Concise, Add Detail, Formal or Friendly Tone, Summarize.

feature 2 / 6

/ commands

Type / in any paragraph for a menu of AI actions and quick blocks. The AI works on that paragraph and shows its result in a card before it touches your text.

AI actions
continueimprovefix grammarshorterlongerformaltranslatesummarizetableask
AI requests
1 per command

how to use it

  1. 1In the paragraph you want to change, type / and a few letters to filter, e.g. /impr.
  2. 2Press Enter. Add words for the commands that take them: /translate hindi, /table monthly budget by team, /ask write an intro.
  3. 3The result streams into a card: click Replace or Insert below.

feature 3 / 6

Studio: a writing team

Pick a profession and a team of AI agents writes the whole document. A lead editor plans the sections, a specialist writes each one in its own craft, reviewers check the draft against their checklist, and flagged sections are revised. You watch it happen.

professions
legal · medical · reports · research · news · articles · social media · film & TV · YouTube · reels · courses
use it when
You need a complete first draft: a contract, a report, a script, a course outline.
AI requests
Draft: 1 + 1 per section
Review and revise: up to 1 + 2 per section + 1 per reviewer

how to use it

  1. 1Click the chat options under the message box (This document · Quick) and choose Write a whole document.
  2. 2Pick a profession on the left and a document type, and answer the brief.
  3. 3Optionally tick source files: facts from them are cited as [1], [2] and listed under Sources.
  4. 4Choose Draft, or Draft, review and revise for the reviewers’ pass, then Write it.
  5. 5When it’s done, Add to my document. If the document isn’t empty, it arrives as a review.

the team

  1. lead editor plans the sections, assigns each one
  2. ↓
  3. specialists each writes its sections in its craft
  4. ↓
  5. reviewers check claims, gaps and clarity
  6. ↓
  7. revise flagged sections rewritten

feature 4 / 6

Deep read · RLM

recursive language model

Normal AI chat only sees the parts of a long document that fit in one prompt. Deep read gives the model the whole text as a variable in a sandbox. It writes small programs to look around (search, slice, split into chunks), hands pieces to helper model calls, and combines what they find. Short texts (under about 24,000 characters) are simply read in one pass.

use it when
“Summarise this 120-page report”, “list every deadline”, “compare section 2 with section 7”.
AI requests
Short texts: 1
Long texts: up to 8 steps + 24 helper reads

how to use it

  1. 1Open the long document.
  2. 2Click the chat options (This document · Quick) and pick Deep read under Reading mode.
  3. 3Ask your question. A line shows each step as it reads.
  4. 4Under the answer, Read with code folds out the exact code it ran and what it printed.

The code runs in a locked sandbox: no files, no network, no imports, with time and size limits.

what a step looks like

step 1 · sandbox

# the model writes this; the sandbox runs it
parts = chunks(context, 20000)
answers = llm_query_batch(
    ["List every deadline:\n" + p for p in parts])
print(len(parts), answers)
# next step: combine the answers and finish

feature 5 / 6

Search · RAG

retrieval-augmented generation

Every file you open or upload is split into passages. When you ask a question, the passages most relevant to it are found first, and only those are given to the AI, numbered. The answer cites them like [1] [2], so you can check every claim against the source.

use it when
“What did we agree on payment terms?”, “which document mentions the Pune office?”
AI requests
1 per question

how to use it

  1. 1Open your files once with File → Open a file (Word, PDF, text).
  2. 2In the chat options, choose All my documents under Reads.
  3. 3Ask. Click a [1] in the answer, or a source under it, to see the exact passage.

In the Windows app, passages are ranked by keyword match, so use the words the document uses.

rag or deep read?

search · rag

A fact that lives in a few passages, across many files. Fast, 1 request.

deep read

A question about the whole of one long document. Thorough, several requests.

feature 6 / 6

Autocomplete

As you pause, the AI suggests how your sentence continues, in grey. Press Tab to take it, or keep typing to ignore it.

use it when
Drafting quickly, when you know where a sentence is going.
AI requests
1 per suggestion

how to use it

  1. 1Open More tools (⋯) in the toolbar and turn on AI autocomplete.
  2. 2Write, and pause at the end of a sentence. Press Tab to accept the suggestion.
  3. 3Each suggestion is a request, so a free key’s per-minute limit runs out faster.

// faq

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, and open source. AI usage is billed by your provider on your own key; Gemini’s free tier costs nothing.

AI says “quota exceeded”.

Free keys allow a few requests a minute. Wait a minute; Studio and deep read make several requests each, so heavy use suits a paid key.

Can I use my account on another PC?

Accounts belong to the PC they were made on. Copy the data folder to move your documents.

Mac or Linux?

Not yet as an installer. The source code runs anywhere Python and Node.js do.

Scanned PDFs?

Not yet in the Windows app: scanned pages import without text. PDFs with real text work.
